BINS, WASTE AND RECYCLING

Understand common waste categories.

Separate common items correctly and check council requirements for your area.

Recycling

Empty and rinse containers where required. Cardboard, paper, bottles and cans may be accepted locally.

Organic waste

Food scraps and suitable garden waste may be collected in an organics service.

Special disposal

Batteries, e-waste, paint and chemicals should never be placed in normal household bins.

  • Keep it clean and dryContaminated recyclables can affect the rest of a recycling load.
  • Do not guess with hazardous itemsUse the Waste Finder and contact a specialist collection service.
  • Check local guidanceBin lid colours, collections and accepted materials vary between councils.
