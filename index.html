ABOUT THE PROJECT

A student-built solution focused on a real community problem.

Our team identified waste sorting and recycling confusion as an opportunity to design a simple digital information platform.

The Problem We Identified

Australian households deal with different types of waste every day, but it is not always obvious whether an item belongs in recycling, organic waste, general waste or a special disposal service.

Waste information may also differ between local council areas. Users can therefore spend time searching through several sources before finding a clear answer.

Our project brings common waste information together in a simple, visual and easy-to-navigate website.

Our Mission

Make waste information easier to understand and more accessible to everyday users.

Our Vision

Support cleaner communities by encouraging informed and responsible waste decisions.

Project Context

WASTE STREAMS

Understanding common waste categories

♻️

Recycling

Suitable materials can potentially be processed and reused instead of entering general waste.

  • Suitable paper and cardboard
  • Accepted glass containers
  • Metal cans
  • Accepted plastic containers
🍃

Organic Waste

Where an organics service exists, suitable food and garden material may be collected separately.

  • Food scraps
  • Fruit and vegetable scraps
  • Grass clippings
  • Suitable garden waste
🗑️

General Waste

Used for materials that are not accepted through available recycling or organics services.

  • Non-recyclable packaging
  • Some mixed materials
  • Damaged household items
  • Other non-recyclable waste
⚠️

Special Disposal

Some items should not be placed in ordinary household bins.

  • Batteries
  • Electronic waste
  • Chemicals
  • Hazardous materials
