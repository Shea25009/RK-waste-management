ABOUT THE PROJECT

Clear information. Better choices.

RK's Waste Management is a student-built waste-information prototype that makes everyday disposal guidance easier to find and understand.

Purpose

Help households identify a sensible next step before placing an item in a bin.

Who it supports

Residents, councils, community groups and anyone seeking simpler waste education.

Our approach

Plain language, meaningful navigation and links to official guidance for final decisions.
