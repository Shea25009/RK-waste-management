Stakeholder Analysis
RK's Waste Management
Australian Residents and Households
They are the main users of the website. They need clear information about recycling and correct waste disposal.
Local Councils
Local councils provide waste collection and recycling services and are important sources of waste management information.
Waste Management Companies
These companies collect, process and manage different types of waste.
Environmental Organisations
Environmental organisations promote recycling, sustainability and responsible waste management practices.
